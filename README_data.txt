
Data files in this folder:


aurorae_Krivsky1996.dat
aurorae_Krivsky1996_notes.txt

    Krivsky 1996 auroral catalogue and comments, mirrored from
    ftp://ftp.ngdc.noaa.gov/STP/space-weather/aurora-airglow/


aurorae_master_nokorean.dat
aurorae_master_notes.txt

    Krivsky's catalogue supplemented with more recently published
    pre-1700 data (except Korean-only observations):


annual_aurorae_nokorean.xlsx
annual_aurorae_nokorean_1500_1650.xlsx

    Annual list of number of days with auroral observations based on 
    aurorae_master_nokorean.dat. 
    As large storms often last ~1 day long, from 2 consecutive days
    only the 1st day is taken into account, from more consecutive days
    only the odd-numbered days.


Berggren_600yrs_NGRIP.txt

    Be-10 data measured in NGRIP ice core as published by 
    Berggren et al.: 
    A 600-year annual 10 Be record from the NGRIP ice core, Greenland. 
    Geophys. Res. Lett. 36, L11801, DOI: 10.1029/2009GL038004 (2009)
    and mirrored from
    ftp://ftp.ncdc.noaa.gov/pub/data/paleo/icecore/
